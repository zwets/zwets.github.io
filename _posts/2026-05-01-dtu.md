After ten years at [Kilimanjaro Clinical Research Institute](https://www.kcri.ac.tz),
and three years as a [bioinformatician without borders](https://io.zwets.it/2023/03/01/bioinformatician-without-borders/),
I am happy to announce that as of today I am a member of Frank Aarestrup's
[Research Group for Genetic Epidemiology](https://www.food.dtu.dk/Forskning/Genetisk-Epidemiologi)
at the [Fødevareinstituttet at Technical University of Denmark (DTU)](https://www.food.dtu.dk).

I am [much looking forward](https://lnkd.in/p/esEavQdx) to working on the ambitious GPAP project,
while also managing the institute's HPC.
